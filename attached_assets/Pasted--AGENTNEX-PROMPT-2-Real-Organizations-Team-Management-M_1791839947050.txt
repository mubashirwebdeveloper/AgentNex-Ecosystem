## 4. ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL (RBAC)

Implement real server-enforced organization permissions.

Use these roles:

**Owner**
- Full organization control
- Manage settings and members
- Change member roles
- Transfer ownership
- Delete the organization when explicitly confirmed

**Admin**
- Manage authorized organization settings
- Invite and manage eligible team members
- Access organization management features
- Cannot perform owner-only operations

**Member**
- Access permitted organization content
- Update their own membership-related profile where supported
- Cannot manage organization security, ownership, or administrative roles

**Viewer**
- Read-only access to permitted organization information
- Cannot modify organization data or manage members

Create a centralized permission-checking mechanism.

**Never enforce permissions through frontend button visibility alone.**

Every protected API operation and database mutation must verify the authenticated user's actual membership and role on the server.

Prevent privilege escalation and cross-organization access.

## 5. TEAM MANAGEMENT & INVITATIONS

Build a functioning team management page.

Authorized users must be able to:

- View real organization members.
- See each member's role and membership status.
- Invite members.
- Choose permitted invitation roles.
- View pending invitations.
- Revoke pending invitations.
- Remove members when authorized.
- Change member roles within permitted boundaries.

Invitations must be secure:

- Use cryptographically secure invitation tokens.
- Store tokens securely, preferably hashed.
- Enforce expiration and single-use acceptance.
- Bind invitations to their intended recipient identity.
- Prevent unauthorized acceptance.
- Prevent duplicates and invitation replay.
- Ensure acceptance is transactional.

Use a configured email delivery provider if one is already available.

If email delivery is not configured, provide a secure copyable invitation link with clear manual-sharing instructions. Do not pretend an email was sent.

An invited user must authenticate before accepting.

## 6. OWNERSHIP & SAFETY RULES

Implement safe ownership management.

- An organization must never accidentally be left without an owner.
- The final owner cannot simply leave or be removed.
- Ownership transfer requires an explicit, authorized action.
- Admins cannot promote themselves to owner.
- Members cannot elevate their own permissions.
- Users cannot modify memberships in organizations they do not belong to.
- Deletion requires explicit confirmation and must respect dependent records.

Do not silently delete organizations or user data.

If permanent organization deletion would create unnecessary risk at this stage, implement a clearly documented restricted or soft-delete strategy instead.
